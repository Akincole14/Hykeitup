Our Story

An Impulse Idea That
Became a Movement

Hyke It Up was an impulse idea our Founder Sable created in April 2024. After searching for local hiking clubs without success, she decided to create one with co-founder Akin.

A community for millennials to meet up once a month across different locations in the U.K to get fit and explore the outdoors while networking and meeting new people.

The community aims to help millennials make friends, build community vibes, and support one another through life's journey — on the trail and beyond.

  • Community-led, community-owned
  • All fitness levels welcome
  • Safety briefings on every hyke
  • No hiking experience needed to join

Hyke Abroad · Lille

A Hyke It Up coach adventure across the Channel — great trails, great company, great night out.

passports ready

🚌 Transport Coach from London
💷 Coach Cost £350 per person
📍 Departure Stratford International
🥾 Hyke Duration 3 – 4 Hours
The Trip

A Weekend to Remember

Join us on our first international Hyke It Up experience as we head to the beautiful city of Lille, France. We'll be travelling together by coach from Stratford International, settling into our hotel in and around Lille Town Centre, lacing up our boots for a stunning 3–4 hour hyke through the French countryside, and finishing the weekend with a well-earned night out before heading home. This is more than a hike — it's a full community adventure.

What to Expect

Weekend Itinerary

Thursday 18th September 2026
🕖 7:00pm Departure

All Aboard at Stratford International

Meet your fellow Hykers at Stratford International station and board the coach heading to Lille, France. Settle in, get to know the group, and enjoy the overnight journey across the Channel.

Friday 19th September 2026
🏨 Arrival — Time TBC

Arrive & Settle In

We'll arrive at Lille Town Centre and check in to our chosen hotels in the surrounding area. Take the day to rest up, explore the city, and soak in the atmosphere of one of France's most vibrant cities.

Saturday 21st September 2026
🕘 9:00am Meet-Up

Hyke Day 🥾

Meet the group at the designated pick-up point at 9am sharp. From there we'll board the coach to our hyke location and take on a 3–4 hour trail through the scenic landscapes of Lille, France — taking in breathtaking views along the way.

3 – 4 hour hyke  ·  Scenic views  ·  All levels welcome
Saturday Evening
🌙 Night Out

Celebrate & Explore

After completing the hyke, we'll head back to our hotels to freshen up before heading out for a brilliant night out in Lille. Good food, good drinks, great company — you've earned it.

Return Journey
🕛 Depart 12:00pm — 20th September

Homeward Bound

After an unforgettable weekend, we'll board the coach back to Stratford International and arrive home with incredible memories and new friendships to last a lifetime.

The Details

What's Included

🚌

Return Coach Travel

Comfortable return coach travel from Stratford International directly to Lille and back.

🏨

Hotel Accommodation

Accommodation in and around Lille Town Centre for the duration of the trip.

🥾

Guided Hyke

A fully guided 3–4 hour hyke through the stunning French countryside with your Hyke It Up crew.

👥

Community Experience

Meet like-minded professionals and build lasting connections over a shared adventure.

* Hotel accommodation costs are separate from the £350 coach transport fee. Full details will be shared with registered attendees.
